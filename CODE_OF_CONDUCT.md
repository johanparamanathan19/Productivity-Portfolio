# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Critique code, not the person who
wrote it.

## What that means here

**Expected:**

- Being welcoming to people regardless of experience, background, or identity.
- Giving feedback on the work rather than the author — "this loses data when
  the import fails" rather than "you clearly didn't think about this".
- Accepting that reasonable people disagree about design, and that the
  maintainer gets the final call on a personal project.
- Owning mistakes and fixing them.

**Not acceptable:**

- Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit messages, and anywhere else
someone is participating in this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem through GitHub — open an issue if it is something that can be
discussed openly, or use the
[Security tab's](https://github.com/johanparamanathan19/Productivity-Portfolio/security)
private reporting if it needs to stay private.

Reports will be reviewed and acted on. Responses range from a request to change
behaviour, through editing or removing comments, to blocking someone from the
repository. The maintainer decides, and will keep the reporter's identity
private.
