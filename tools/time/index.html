      What this says

        A year is treated as a fixed budget of 8,760 hours (365 days), the same way the affordability calculator treats your pay as a fixed budget of money. Your hourly rate comes from your own Work hours and salary, not a claimed statistic about anyone else — this tool has no data about typical time use, only arithmetic on what you enter. Sleep, rest, and downtime overlap in real life more than any set of categories can capture; treat the breakdown as a rough self-audit, not a precise ledger. Nothing you type leaves your device.
