    What this means

      Every figure is inflation-adjusted, so it is in today's money. The return is a long-run historical average for a broad stock index, not a prediction — real returns arrive unevenly, and a bad decade can last a decade. Depreciation rates are rules of thumb by category, not measurements of your specific item. This tool deliberately says nothing about whether the purchase is worth it to you, only what it costs; it is not financial advice. Nothing you type leaves your device.
