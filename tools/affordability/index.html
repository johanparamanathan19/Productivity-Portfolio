      These are widely used rules of thumb — an emergency fund of 3–6 months, the 28/36 debt-to-income rule, 50/30/20 budgeting, the 30% housing threshold, and 20/4/10 for vehicles — applied to take-home rather than gross pay, which makes them stricter than a lender would be.
