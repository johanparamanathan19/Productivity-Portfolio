🪜 Debt Escape Plan

Debt is compounding working against you instead of for you — the same arithmetic as investing, aimed the other way. List what you owe, say what you can put toward it each month, and this charts the two standard payoff orders side by side: highest rate first, which always costs the least, against smallest balance first, which is easier to actually finish.

Before the plan

  1. Stop adding to it. Every figure below assumes no new spending joins the balances you're paying down.
  2. Never miss a minimum. A missed payment can trigger a default rate far above what you're paying now — this plan pays every minimum before it optimises anything else.
  3. A small buffer first. Roughly a month of essentials, so the next surprise expense doesn't land straight back on a card. Check with How Long Could I Last?

          What this means

            Every figure here is nominal — the same units as your statements, not adjusted for inflation, which is a deliberate difference from this site's investing tools. Interest compounds monthly in this model rather than daily, which is close but not exact. This does not recommend any loan, balance transfer, or consolidation product, and it is not financial or credit counselling. Nothing you type leaves your device.
